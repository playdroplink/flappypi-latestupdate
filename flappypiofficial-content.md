## 🎤 Scream Pi - Voice-Activated Pi Gaming Adventure

### Game Overview
The world's first voice-controlled Pi Network game! Use your voice to control characters, activate special abilities, and navigate through challenging levels. Scream, shout, or speak commands to progress and earn Pi rewards.

### Key Features
- **Voice Recognition Technology**: Control the game with your voice
- **Multiple Control Modes**: 
  - **Scream Mode**: Use your voice to control the character
  - **Tap Mode**: Traditional touch controls
  - **Both Modes**: Combine voice and touch for maximum control
- **Unlockable Characters**: Each character has unique abilities and special moves
- **Dynamic Weather Systems**: Different weather affects gameplay
- **Real-time Voice Processing**: Instant voice command recognition
- **Pi Network Integration**: Earn real Pi cryptocurrency
- **Character Evolution**: Unlock and upgrade characters
- **Weather Effects**: Rain, snow, wind, and more affect gameplay

### Voice Commands
- **"Jump"** or **"Up"**: Make the character jump
- **"Scream"** or **"Shout"**: Activate special abilities
- **"Shield"**: Activate protective shield
- **"Magnet"**: Activate coin magnet
- **"Recovery"**: Heal the character

### How to Play
1. Open Scream Pi in Pi Browser
2. Grant microphone permissions
3. Choose your control mode (Scream, Tap, or Both)
4. Use your voice or tap to control the character
5. Navigate through obstacles and collect rewards
6. Unlock new characters and abilities

### Characters
- **Bobman**: Balanced character with good all-around stats
- **Chengdiao**: High jump specialist
- **Nicolas**: Shield and defense expert
